# Hi, I'm Kevin Kamau

Founder and lead engineer at **[CodeNova](https://codenovaone.com)**, a Nairobi studio that designs and builds websites, online stores and custom software for businesses in Kenya and around the world, with M-Pesa and card payments built in.

I'm also studying Software Engineering at Kirinyaga University.

## Work in production

| Project | What we built | Live |
|---|---|---|
| **Minada** | A multi-store SaaS: every business gets its own shop, POS and subscription plan, and is paid directly by M-Pesa or card | [minada.ke](https://minada.ke) |
| **Stationery Central** | Multi-store e-commerce with M-Pesa paid straight to each seller | [stationerycentraldepot.ke](https://stationerycentraldepot.ke) |
| **Family Peace Association Kenya** | Events, donations by M-Pesa and PayPal, and an admin panel | [familypeaceassociationkenya.org](https://familypeaceassociationkenya.org) |
| **Tetezi Properties** | Custom property website with SEO and ongoing care | [teteziproperties.co.ke](https://teteziproperties.co.ke) |
| **First Interiors Designs** | A luxury portfolio for a Nairobi interior design studio | [case study](https://codenovaone.com/work#first-interiors) |

More case studies at **[codenovaone.com/work](https://codenovaone.com/work)**.
